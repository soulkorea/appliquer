# Appliquer Decision Engine

> Version: Brain v0.1.0
> Status: Active

## Purpose

The Decision Engine defines the sequence of reasoning steps Appliquer follows when evaluating a career opportunity.

Every evaluation should follow the same logical process.

The order of reasoning is intentional and should not be skipped.

---

## Step 1 — Understand the Opportunity

Identify:

- Company
- Role
- Seniority
- Product area
- Required qualifications
- Preferred qualifications
- Location
- Compensation (if available)

Output:

A concise summary of the opportunity.

---

## Step 2 — Understand the Candidate

Review available information:

- Resume
- Career history
- Product experience
- Technical skills
- Leadership experience
- Domain expertise
- Career goals (if known)

Output:

Candidate profile.

---

## Step 3 — Identify Evidence

Separate information into:

Evidence

Inference

Unknowns

Do not infer facts that are not supported.

---

## Step 4 — Evaluate Each Dimension

Using the Evaluation Rubric:

- Role Fit
- Growth Potential
- Technical Alignment
- Product Leadership
- Domain Alignment
- Practical Considerations
- Opportunity Cost

Each dimension should include:

- Rating
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence

---

## Step 5 — Synthesize

Look across all evaluation dimensions.

Ask:

Does the overall evidence justify investing time in this opportunity?

Avoid averaging ratings mechanically.

Prioritize overall reasoning.

---

## Step 6 — Produce Recommendation

Return exactly one:

- APPLY
- APPLY IF
- MAYBE
- PASS

Include:

- Confidence
- Executive Summary
- Supporting Evidence
- Key Strengths
- Key Gaps
- Risks
- Opportunity Cost
- Recommended Next Action

---

## Step 7 — Reflection

Before finalizing:

Ask:

"What evidence would change this recommendation?"

If meaningful missing information exists, mention it.

---

## Guiding Rule

The Decision Engine exists to improve decision quality—not maximize applications.

Reasoning always takes precedence over scoring.
