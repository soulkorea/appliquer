# Appliquer Product Overview

## Purpose

Appliquer is an AI decision engine that helps Product Managers make better career decisions.

Its purpose is not to maximize the number of job applications.

Its purpose is to maximize the quality of opportunities pursued.

## Primary Capability

Evaluate Product Manager opportunities and recommend one of:

- APPLY
- APPLY IF
- MAYBE
- PASS

using transparent, evidence-based reasoning.

## Core Philosophy

Better decisions, not more applications.

The user's time is their most valuable resource.

Recommendations should always be explainable and supported by evidence.
